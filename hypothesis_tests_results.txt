STATISTICAL BUSINESS ANALYSIS
HYPOTHESIS TEST RESULTS
========================================

Dataset: Business Sales Data
Number of Observations: 100
Significance Level: 0.05


--------------------------------------------------
HYPOTHESIS TEST 1: ONE-SAMPLE T-TEST
--------------------------------------------------

Business Question:
Is the average Total_Sales significantly different from ₹100,000?

Null Hypothesis (H₀):
The population mean Total_Sales is equal to ₹100,000.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):
The population mean Total_Sales is different from ₹100,000.

Benchmark Mean: ₹100,000.00
Sample Mean: ₹123,650.48

t-statistic: 2.3612
p-value: 0.020172

Decision:
Reject H₀ because p-value < 0.05.

Conclusion:
There is statistically significant evidence that the average
Total_Sales differs from ₹100,000. The observed sample mean
of ₹123,650.48 is higher than the benchmark.


--------------------------------------------------
HYPOTHESIS TEST 2: INDEPENDENT TWO-SAMPLE WELCH T-TEST
--------------------------------------------------

Business Question:
Is the average Total_Sales significantly different between
the North and South regions?

Null Hypothesis (H₀):
The mean Total_Sales of North and South regions is equal.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):
The mean Total_Sales of North and South regions is different.

North Sample Size: 28
South Sample Size: 27

North Average Total_Sales: ₹142,272.68
South Average Total_Sales: ₹138,438.96

t-statistic: 0.1299
p-value: 0.897152

Decision:
Fail to reject H₀ because p-value > 0.05.

Conclusion:
There is insufficient statistical evidence to conclude that
the average Total_Sales differs between the North and South
regions.


--------------------------------------------------
HYPOTHESIS TEST 3: ONE-WAY ANOVA
--------------------------------------------------

Business Question:
Is there a statistically significant difference in average
Total_Sales among East, North, South, and West regions?

Null Hypothesis (H₀):
The mean Total_Sales is equal across all four regions.

Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):
At least one region has a different mean Total_Sales.

Regional Average Total_Sales:

East:  ₹132,612.58
North: ₹142,272.68
South: ₹138,438.96
West:  ₹81,689.31

F-statistic: 2.1644
p-value: 0.097237

Decision:
Fail to reject H₀ because p-value > 0.05.

Conclusion:
There is insufficient statistical evidence to conclude that
average Total_Sales differs significantly among the four
regions.

--------------------------------------------------
OVERALL CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------

The statistical analysis provides evidence of important
relationships within the sales data.

Quantity and Price are positively associated with Total_Sales.
The average Total_Sales is significantly different from the
₹100,000 benchmark.

However, the analysis did not find statistically significant
differences in average sales between North and South regions
or among all four regions.

The results can help businesses understand sales patterns,
evaluate performance, and identify areas for further analysis.
Additional variables such as marketing expenditure, discounts,
product category, customer characteristics, and seasonality
could improve future statistical and regression analysis.
